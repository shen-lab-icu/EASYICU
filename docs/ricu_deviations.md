# Deviations from ricu 0.6.3

EasyICU follows ricu 0.6.3 concept definitions unless a row below says
otherwise. Each row names the concept, the databases it reaches, what ricu
does, what EasyICU does, why, and the measured effect. Counts of 1–9 are
written as "<10". "Sample" means the 3,000-stay server sample of that database
used for the lead-r13 gates; "demo" means the public PhysioNet demo.

Per-commit details are in [extraction_changelog.md](extraction_changelog.md).

The s732ricuc measurements above use the supplied ricu 0.6.3 gold and thesis
seal `21f703c48`, with each concept's matched stays. Counts are floored to tens;
counts below ten are suppressed. Raw MIMIC-IV checks additionally join the
task's allowed-member relation and can cover fewer stays than the export/gold
comparison. The audit changed no code: the zero-span grouped duration it
proposed to align with ricu stays as declared in DEV-RICUC-06. The concept
dictionary and its seal did not change.
