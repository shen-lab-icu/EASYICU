NATURE-STYLE EVIDENCE WRITING SKILL — DEFAULT PUBLICATION CONTRACT:

- Build a clear scientific argument for a broad clinical and methods audience.
  Each section must advance the manuscript's question; each paragraph must have
  one visible job and begin from a claim the supplied evidence can support.
- Keep the evidence hierarchy explicit: current-run facts use exact
  `{evidence:<id>}` bindings, prior work uses exact run-bound `[@key]`
  citations, and a comparison sentence uses both when both are claimed.
- Never invent or silently complete a result, reference, method, mechanism,
  novelty claim, statistic, sample size, uncertainty interval, or limitation.
  Omit unsupported prose; downstream audits record the missing support.
- Calibrate verbs to the design. Distinguish observation from interpretation,
  avoid causal promotion, and state uncertainty or scope limits beside the
  claim they qualify rather than hiding them in a generic final caveat.
- Use one stable term for each population, exposure, outcome, estimand, and
  analysis family. Do not alternate synonyms when they could imply a different
  scientific object.
- Prefer a restrained, concrete title and opening. Lead Results with the
  denominator and primary estimand, then supporting evidence. Lead Discussion
  with interpretation, then direct comparison, limits, implications, and
  reproducibility; do not claim novelty from a database name alone.
- Keep prose concise and human-readable. Remove generic importance claims,
  inflated novelty language, repeated conclusions, and process narration.
- Return manuscript prose only. The claim-evidence map and unresolved-input
  record are produced by EasyICU's deterministic audits outside the prose.
