  revision justified by probe or completed evidence. Do not add this roster to
  survival, prediction, mixed-effects, clustering, or other families whose
  contracts it does not represent.
- If the probe or completed steps reveal missingness artefacts, component
  completeness failures, outcome mismatches, or impossible cross-database assumptions,
  insert targeted audit or protocol steps rather than ignoring the issue.
- Do not drop a completed step from the returned plan. Preserve completed
  steps exactly as they were and increment the plan revision.
